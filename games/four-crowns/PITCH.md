# Pitch: Four Crowns

- **Slug:** four-crowns
- **Date:** 2026-11-08
- **One-line hook:** Rule a floating island kingdom: draft element cards, place buildings on a tiny grid, and watch your army auto-battle waves of invaders, building a run-defining combo before the boss arrives.

## Genre & references
Roguelike deckbuilder + auto-battler + kingdom builder. Mechanically inspired by *9 Kings* (grid placement, card-per-round, auto-resolved battles). Inspiration only: no names, content or look copied.

## Core loop
1. Draw a hand of cards (buildings, edicts, decrees).
2. Spend gold to place/upgrade buildings on your 3×3 kingdom grid; adjacency creates synergies.
3. Battle: buildings spawn units that auto-fight the incoming wave lane by lane; you may cast decrees.
4. Pick a reward card; next round is harder; survive the boss.

## The player feels...
Like a clever ruler whose small decisions snowball into a satisfying, slightly broken combo.

## Session length / structure
Vertical slice: one run = 5 rounds incl. a boss, ~8–10 minutes.

## Factions (original setting: the Shattered Reach)
- **Ember Throne** (Fire): aggressive burn and sacrifice
- **Stone Court** (Earth): fortress and slow scaling
- **Gale Covenant** (Air): tempo swarms and repositioning
- **Tide Circle** (Water): sustain and transform combos

## Must-haves (slice)
One playable faction, 3×3 grid, ~10 cards, 5 rounds, 1 boss, data-driven content schema for all four factions.

## Nice-to-haves
Battle speed toggle, run summary with score.

## Out of scope (slice)
Other playable factions, meta progression, grid expansion, mobile layout.

## Platform / input
Web (desktop browser), mouse + keyboard.
